Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Your privacy is paramount. Read how data is handled across our applications and platforms.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect any personal identifier statistics or tracking telemetry within our core products, including the BeatWave and WaveMirror applications. Any user inputs entered into the Ask Me Anything AI assistant interface are cached on your local runtime client (LocalStorage) and are not stored permanently on remote database collections.

2. Contact and handshake Gateway

When you submit a contact inquiry handshake form on our portal, the name, email, phone number, and message text details are routed over secure SSL interfaces to our integrated Discord webhooks for instant administrative notifications. This data is used solely to calibrate client relations and project alignment discussions and is never shared with third-party tracking indexes.

3. Application Permissions

BeatWave APK requires standard local audio background play runtimes. It operates entirely open-source without network telemetry tracking, background analytics trackers, or commercial advertising modules.

4. Security Protocols

All communication channels are encrypted under standard secure sockets configurations. Database integrations deployed for client projects under Zephyr Devs run on secured serverless and hosting stacks (Vercel, Netlify, Firebase) with strict firewall routing rules.
